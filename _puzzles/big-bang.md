---
title: "Big Bang"
order: 12
rules:
  - name: "Classic Sudoku"
    desc: "Every row, column and 3x3 box must contain every number from 1 to 9."
  - name: "Region Sum Lines"
    desc: "Box borders divide a blue line into multiple segments which have the same sum. Different lines may have different sums."
  - name: "Arrows"
    desc: "Numbers along an arrow sum up to the total in the circled cell."
  - name: "Kropki"
    desc: "Two digits separated by a white dot are consecutive; two digits separated by a black dot have a 1:2 ratio."
  - name: "V"
    desc: "Two digits separated by a V clue sum up to 5. If a clue is on the outer border, it applies to the digits at the two ends of that row/column."
